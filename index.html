📱 Mobile App & Offline Setup

This Beads Counter is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can save it directly to your home screen to use it full-screen and offline anywhere:

On iPhone / iPad (Safari):
  1. Tap the Share button at bottom of browser.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top right.
On Android (Chrome):
  1. Tap the three vertical dots menu icon (⋮).
  2. Tap Install App or Add to Home Screen.
